Questions about Guide.

Guide is human 1:1 coaching. Flow is always-on AI coaching for everyone. Signal is the measurement layer that reports what both produced.

A short goals intake matches on domain, style, and timezone. Members meet two or three coaches and choose their fit — and can switch at any point.

Notes and transcripts stay between member and coach. Attune holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, with GDPR compliance and regional data residency.

Assessment movement is paired with your own business metrics by cohort, so the report reads in the same units your finance team already uses.
